Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Evansville HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Evansville HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Evansville and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.
